## Recommended Books

| Book | Author(s) | Focus Area |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| *Clean Architecture* | Robert C. Martin | SOLID, Dependency Rule, Architecture |
| *Design Patterns* | Gamma, Helm, Johnson, Vlissides (GoF) | 23 classical patterns |
| *Domain-Driven Design* | Eric Evans | Strategic & tactical DDD |
| *Implementing Domain-Driven Design* | Vaughn Vernon | Practical DDD |
| *Refactoring* | Martin Fowler | Incremental improvement, reducing debt |
| *Building Evolutionary Architectures* | Ford, Parsons, Kua | Architecture fitness functions, managing change |
| *Accelerate* | Forsgren, Humble, Kim | Engineering metrics, technical leadership |
| *The Staff Engineer's Path* | Tanya Reilly | Technical leadership beyond the manager track |
| *Software Engineering at Google* | Winters, Manshreck, Wright | Testing, code review, large-scale practices |

> **Final thought for the Principal Engineer:**
>
> Your impact is no longer measured by lines of code written. It is measured by the **clarity of direction** you set, the **quality of decisions** you facilitate, the **technical culture** you cultivate, and the **sustainability** of the systems you shepherd. Master these fundamentals — not as academic knowledge, but as daily practice — and you amplify the output of every engineer around you.
